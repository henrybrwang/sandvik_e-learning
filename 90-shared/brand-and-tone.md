# Brand And Tone

## Writing Tone

Use a tone that is:

- Clear
- Practical
- Calm
- Direct
- Respectful of global English readers
- Specific about user responsibility

Avoid:

- Hype
- Fear-based language
- Unsupported productivity claims
- Jokes that may not translate
- Product promises that Sandvik has not approved

## Content Style

- Use short sections and clear headings.
- Prefer examples over abstract principles.
- Explain acronyms the first time they appear.
- Mark Sandvik policy placeholders visibly.
- Separate "Claude can" from "Sandvik allows".

## Visual Style

Until Brand approves final guidance:

- Treat all generated visuals as drafts.
- Use placeholder labels for Sandvik logos, colors, typography, and imagery.
- Do not create external-facing material without Brand and Legal review.
- Include alt text for any visual asset used in exported learning material.

## Accessibility

- Use plain English.
- Avoid color-only meaning.
- Ensure screenshots and visuals have written descriptions.
- Keep quiz questions concise.
- Make completion instructions explicit.

## Standard Module Notice

Use this notice in each module:

> Claude can help accelerate work, but users remain responsible for what they enter, approve, share, and publish. Follow Sandvik policy, protect sensitive information, and review outputs before relying on them.
